## Design Context

### Users

People arriving from community-board links, with mobile users as the majority.
They want to understand what each app does quickly, open the correct download
destination, and report a problem without hunting through the site.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, plain, trustworthy. A text-first catalog with open books on support and
spending, not a dashboard or a promotional ad wall.

### Aesthetic Direction

White page, single 680px column, Pretendard, grayscale OKLCH tokens, thin
dividers. No illustration, no decoration beyond hierarchy and spacing. Source of
truth: Claude Design `Homepage.dc.html`.

### Design Principles

1. Every app is visible and reachable with no interaction required.
2. Mobile-first single column; never a horizontal scroll.
3. Factual app data comes from `shipping.yml`; support data from `data/support.ts`.
4. No fabricated numbers: empty data renders an empty state.
