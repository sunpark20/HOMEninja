## Design Context

### Users

People arriving from community-board links, with mobile users as the majority.
They want to understand what each app does quickly, open the correct download
destination, and report a problem without hunting through the site. Returning
visitors may optionally keep a small local resident identity in their browser.

### Brand Personality

Handmade, playful, welcoming. The site should feel like a small illustrated
village with a helpful host, not like a dashboard or a promotional ad wall.

### Aesthetic Direction

Bright daytime countryside and a tactile storybook surface: paper warmth,
drawn signboards, trees, leaves, a mailbox, a house, and restrained hard
shadows. The memorable interaction is that app leaves can fall from the
platform trees into an easy-to-scan ground layout. The anti-reference is the
retired dark cosmic/galaxy interface and generic AI landing-page decoration.

### Design Principles

1. Make the ten apps discoverable and downloadable even with motion disabled.
2. Design mobile-first as a vertical village, then give desktop more breathing
   room without introducing horizontal scrolling.
3. Keep factual app data sourced from `shipping.yml`; visual placement and
   illustration are separate presentation data.
4. Use warm, solid, accessible OKLCH tokens with purposeful hand-drawn detail,
   not gradients in text, glass effects, or decorative UI repetition.
5. Keep resident personalization local-only: no email collection and no
   network-backed signup flow.
